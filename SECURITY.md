# Reporting a security problem

If you find a security problem in Ugo, please tell us privately at **security@ugo.ng**. Do not open a public issue.

Please include what you found, the steps to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. We will reply within 3 working days, keep you updated, and tell you when it is fixed.

Please do not access, change or delete other people's data, do not run denial of service tests, and do not use social engineering against our staff or users. If you follow these rules and act in good faith, we will not take legal action against you.

The same page is published on the website at https://ugo.ng/security.
